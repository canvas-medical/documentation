{% include alert.html type="danger" content="With great power comes great responsibility. Your compute() method runs synchronously inside a worker. A slow compute() — especially one that makes a blocking network call to an external service (an LLM API, a third-party HTTP endpoint) — holds that worker for its entire duration. If the external service slows down or goes offline, every event that triggers your handler piles up until the worker pool is exhausted, and your entire Canvas instance can grind to a halt and stop responding to requests. Long timeouts and retries multiply the damage: a 300 second timeout retried three times pins a single worker for over ten minutes. Keep compute() fast, and move long-running or failure-prone work off the synchronous path — see Keep compute() fast." %}
