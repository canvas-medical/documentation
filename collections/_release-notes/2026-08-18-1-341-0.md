Today's release includes the following updates:
<span class="tag-bugfix">bugfix</span>

- When reviewing a lab report from the panel view, the "Send via patient app" communication method now appears, matching the behavior that already worked on the patient chart. It is also correctly limited to lab reviews, so it no longer shows on imaging, referral, or uncategorized document reviews.
- Internal only, no customer-visible change. The pharmacy service no longer accepts a DrFirst source-id registration without an organization id, so a customer can no longer be recorded as EPCS-configured with an organization id that does not exist at DrFirst.
- Providers on a touch screen can now act on a medication from the patient chart. Tapping a medication in the chart list opens its detail, where Stop, Adjust, and Refill are full-size buttons and the medication name stays readable. On a mouse or trackpad, including an iPad with a keyboard attached, the buttons continue to appear on the row on hover.

<span class="tag-sdk">sdk</span>

- n.a.
- Plugins can now manage a patient's contacts — emergency contacts, next-of-kin, and other related persons — through the [Patient](/sdk/effect-patient/) effect. A contact either holds the person's details inline, or references another Canvas patient, which is how two patients are linked to each other. [PatientContactPerson](/sdk/data-patient-contact-person/) exposes a patient's contacts for reading, and [ContactCategory](/sdk/data-contact-category/) lists the relationship codings the instance defines — look one up there before writing it, since a coding the instance does not have is rejected rather than created.
- Fixed an issue in electronic prescribing of controlled substances (EPCS) that could block enrollment for prescribers with a DEA number and, in some cases, revoke an already-enrolled prescriber's controlled-substance access. Also tightened authentication on the pharmacy EPCS and directory endpoints.
