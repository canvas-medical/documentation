Today's release includes the following updates:

<span class="tag-sdk">sdk</span>

- Plugins can now give the patient portal your brand's look, starting from the login page: your colors, font, and corner radius, and the word the portal uses for the person (patient, member, or client). Signed-in pages can also take your own CSS, and each business line can have its own theme. Portals without a theme plugin look the same as before. [Theming the Patient Portal](/guides/theme-the-patient-portal/) walks through each setting, and the [`PatientPortalTheme`](/sdk/patient-portal/#theme-the-patient-portal) effect has the reference.
- New example plugin: [Appointment Copay Collection](/sdk/example-appointment_copay_collection/) lets patients pay an upcoming appointment's copay with a saved card from the patient portal.

<span class="tag-config">config</span>

- [`PRINT_ROS_AS_RECORDED`](/sdk/commands/#printed-output) prints the [Review of Systems](/sdk/commands/#review-of-systems) exactly as recorded: each question with its selected answers, without the **Reports:** label or the **Denies:** line. Chart search results use the same format. Reach out to Canvas support to enable it for your instance.

<span class="tag-bugfix">bugfix</span>

- Fixed an issue where Refill and Adjust from the chart's Medications list prefilled the directions from the last prescription, missing a newer Change Medication. Both now prefill the medication's current directions, the same as a Refill added from inside a note.
- Fixed issues in population health and the schedule:
  - Choosing a protocol in population health could briefly show a **Start campaign** button that opened a broken page.
  - Clicking a filter, such as **My tasks**, right after the schedule loaded could send you back to the schedule.
  - A link to start a campaign for a protocol that no longer exists opens the populations list.
- Fixed an issue where staff who had never set a password, such as accounts created through an integration, received no email from **Forgot password** on instances without SSO. They can now set a password from the login page.
- Fixed an issue where **Send Reset Password Link** in Settings failed for a staff member with no primary practice location.
- The [Audit Report](https://help.canvasmedical.com/articles/7194605474-Report-Jobs) in Settings names the admin who created a staff account or changed its permissions, rather than the account that was changed.
