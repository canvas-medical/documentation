Today's release includes the following updates:

<span class="tag-portal">portal</span> <span class="tag-sdk">sdk</span>

- Make the patient portal match your brand! Plugins can now theme the whole portal, starting at the login page:
  - Your brand colors on buttons, links, the menu, and the page background.
  - Your font, with corners as rounded or square as you like.
  - Your word for the people you care for: patients, members, or clients.
  - Your own CSS on signed-in pages.
  - A different theme for each business line.

  Start with [Theming the Patient Portal](/guides/theme-the-patient-portal/), or go straight to the [`PATIENT_PORTAL__GET_THEME_CONFIGURATION`](/sdk/events/#patient_portal__get_theme_configuration) event and the [`PatientPortalTheme`](/sdk/patient-portal/#theme-the-patient-portal) effect. Portals without a theme plugin keep their current look.
- New example plugin: [Appointment Copay Collection](/sdk/example-appointment_copay_collection/) lets patients pay an upcoming appointment's copay with a saved card from the patient portal. It brings together:
  - A [portal menu application](/sdk/handlers-applications/#application-scopes) that opens the copay page.
  - A [SimpleAPI](/sdk/handlers-simple-api-http/#patient-session) page that only the signed-in patient can reach.
  - The [`ChargeStoredCard`](/sdk/effect-charge-stored-card/#payment-allocation) effect, which records the payment as the copay on the appointment's claim.
  - The [`REVENUE__STORED_CARD__CHARGE_RESPONSE`](/sdk/events/#stored-card-charge-events) event and a [WebSocket](/sdk/handlers-simple-api-websocket/), which show the patient the result as soon as the charge finishes.

<span class="tag-config">config</span>

- New Constance config [`PRINT_ROS_AS_RECORDED`](/sdk/commands/#printed-output) that prints the [Review of Systems](/sdk/commands/#review-of-systems) exactly as recorded, showing each question with only its selected answers.
  - The printout leaves out the **Reports:** label and the **Denies:** line.
  - The Review of Systems in chart search results matches the printout.
  - Reach out to Canvas support to enable it for your instance. See [Command | Review of Systems](https://help.canvasmedical.com/articles/9046024531-command-review-of-systems).

<span class="tag-data">data</span>

- Security audit events sent to your SIEM now carry more detail. See the [event format](/guides/audit-logging-and-telemetry/#event-format).
  - The instance that produced the event, and whether it's production or non-production.
  - The names of the fields an update changed. Values are never included.
  - The plugin and handler behind a change.

<span class="tag-bugfix">bugfix</span>

- Fixed an issue where Refill and Adjust from the chart's Medications list prefilled the directions from the last prescription, missing a newer Change Medication. Both now prefill the medication's current directions, the same as a Refill added from inside a note.
- Fixed the **Forgot password** link for staff created through the [FHIR Practitioner API](/api/practitioner/), so they can set their password for the first time on instances that don't use SSO.
- Fixed an issue where **Send Reset Password Link** in Settings didn't send the link for staff with no primary practice location set.
- The [Audit Report](https://help.canvasmedical.com/articles/7194605474-Report-Jobs) in Settings names the admin who created a staff account or changed its permissions, rather than the account that was changed. See [Audit Report export](/guides/audit-logging-and-telemetry/#audit-report-export).
