Today's release includes the following updates:

<span class="tag-bugfix">bugfix</span>

- Fixed an issue where side-panel actions such as Refill, Assess, Stop, and Mark as reviewed created a new Chart Review note instead of adding their command to the note the provider was working in. These actions now add to the current note, including when the provider is working in a note application tab.
- Fixed the patient header styling in the appointment popover, reschedule modal, and patient Documents page for easier reading.

<span class="tag-portal">portal</span>

- Patients who share an email address or phone number, such as family members, now log in to the patient portal with their own username and password. The shared email or phone number can't be used to log in, and the login page reminds them to use their username.
