Today's release brings **family portal access** to Canvas! Parents, guardians, adult children, and caregivers can now help manage a loved one's care right from the patient portal: booking visits, messaging the care team, checking lab results, and paying bills, all with their own login. See [Family Portal Access](https://help.canvasmedical.com/articles/1380397114-family-portal-access) to get started.

- **For families:**
  - **Care is a family affair.** A parent, guardian, adult child, or caregiver gets their own portal login for a patient's chart, so they can help without sharing the patient's password.
  - **One login for the whole family.** A parent who is also a patient switches between their own record and each child's.
  - **Everyone stays in the loop.** Family members are notified about new messages, lab results, statements, and appointment reminders in the areas they can see.
  - **Safe and transparent.** Family members confirm the patient's date of birth before opening a chart, and patients can see who has access to their record.
- **For your staff:**
  - **You decide what they see.** From the patient's **Profile → Portal**, give a family member access and set appointments, messaging, clinical, and billing to None, View, or Full.
  - **Always clear who did what.** Messages, bookings, and cancellations from a family member read as them acting for the patient in the inbox, the timeline, and the note footer, such as "Jordan Rivera, for Maya".
  - **Every chart at a glance.** A patient's Portal section lists the other charts their own login can open through family portal access, each linked for staff to open.
  - **Staff follow-up.** If a family member says they don't know the patient, the care team lead gets a task to review the access.
- **Turning it on:** new Constance config `FAMILY_PORTAL_ACCESS` turns on family portal access. Reach out to Canvas support to enable it for your instance.
  - **Before you turn it on, review the plugins you use in the patient portal.** Canvas's built-in pages follow each family member's access, and your plugins need an update to do the same.
    - A plugin's pages and APIs serve family members once the plugin declares the access each one needs. Until then, they serve the patient only.
    - A plugin's widgets, menu items, and applications appear for family members just as they do for the patient, whatever access the family member has.
- **For plugin developers:** build family-aware portal experiences. Greet a parent with their child's next appointment, hide a balance reminder from someone without billing access, or keep a page just for the patient. See [Family Portal Access](/sdk/patient-portal/#family-portal-access).
  - **Data:** read the [portal access a family member has](/sdk/data-portal-access-grant/) on a chart, area by area.
  - **SimpleAPI and WebSocket:** see [who is acting on the chart](/sdk/handlers-simple-api-http/#family-portal-access) and declare the access each route needs.
  - **Applications:** tell the patient from a family member in a [portal menu application](/sdk/handlers-applications/#who-sees-an-application).
- **FHIR API:** a family member acting through family portal access is named as their **RelatedPerson**.
  - [Communication](/api/communication/): a message's `sender` can be a RelatedPerson, for read, search, and create.
  - [Provenance](/api/provenance/): `agent.who` can be a RelatedPerson.
