Today's release brings **family portal access** to Canvas! Parents, guardians, adult children, and caregivers can now help manage a loved one's care right from the patient portal: booking visits, messaging the care team, checking lab results, and paying bills, all with their own login. See [Family Portal Access](https://help.canvasmedical.com/articles/1380397114-family-portal-access) to get started.

<span class="tag-portal">portal</span>

- **Care is a family affair.** Give a parent, guardian, adult child, or caregiver their own portal login for a patient's chart, so they can help without sharing the patient's password.
- **You decide what they see.** From the patient's **Profile → Portal**, set appointments, messaging, clinical, and billing to None, View, or Full for each family member.
- **One login for the whole family.** A parent who is also a patient switches between their own record and each child's. Staff can see every chart a patient's login opens in that patient's Portal section.
- **Everyone stays in the loop.** Family members are notified about new messages, lab results, statements, and appointment reminders in the areas they can see.
- **Always clear who did what.** Messages, bookings, and cancellations from a family member read as them acting for the patient, such as "Jordan Rivera, for Maya".
- **Safeguards built in.** Family members confirm the patient's date of birth before opening a chart, and patients can see who has access to their record. If a family member says they don't know the patient, the care team lead gets a task to review the access.

<span class="tag-config">config</span>

- New Constance config `FAMILY_PORTAL_ACCESS` that turns on family portal access.
  - Ready to get started? Reach out to Canvas support to enable it for your instance.

<span class="tag-sdk">sdk</span>

- **Build family-aware portal experiences.** Plugins can greet a parent with their child's next appointment, hide a balance reminder from someone without billing access, or keep a page just for the patient. See [Family Portal Access](/sdk/patient-portal/#family-portal-access).
  - **Data:** read the [portal access a family member has](/sdk/data-portal-access-grant/) on a chart, area by area.
  - **SimpleAPI and WebSocket:** see [who is acting on the chart](/sdk/handlers-simple-api-http/#family-portal-access) and declare the access each route needs.
  - **Applications:** tell the patient from a family member in a [portal menu application](/sdk/handlers-applications/#who-sees-an-application).

<span class="tag-api">api</span>

- The FHIR API names a family member acting through family portal access as their **RelatedPerson**.
  - [Communication](/api/communication/): a message's `sender` can be a RelatedPerson, for read, search, and create.
  - [Provenance](/api/provenance/): `agent.who` can be a RelatedPerson.
