Today's release includes the following updates:
<span class="tag-bugfix">bugfix</span>

- The family portal access levels explanation in a patient's Portal section now matches what each portal page allows, including the My Health, Labs and Health Records pages under Clinical.

<span class="tag-sdk">sdk</span>

- When a family member uses the patient portal for someone else (family portal access), staff now see who did it. Their messages in the inbox and timeline, and the bookings and cancellations in the note footer, read "Jordan Rivera, for Maya".
- A patient contact's relationship, such as mother or spouse, is picked in its Categories, and family portal access shows that category.
- When a family member with portal access says they don't know the patient, staff now get a task for that patient, assigned to the care team lead, which completes once the access is corrected or removed.
- The staff app loads faster for customers who don't use Zus integrations, because the Zus code no longer downloads for them. Customers with Zus turned on may see a short one-time delay while it loads.
- Two small database tables gain primary keys so instances can move to a newer PostgreSQL version with logical replication. Plugin installs, upgrades, and namespace changes can be paused during database maintenance. No user-facing behavior change otherwise.
- When staff remove a contact that has or had family portal access, the contact leaves the chart but stays on record, so the access history and the records that family member entered still name them.
- Family members with portal access get invites and notices at every email and phone number they have, rather than by one channel the practice picks. They are also told when there is a new message, lab results, a statement, or an appointment reminder on a chart they can see that part of.
- A patient's Profile now lists, in its Portal section, the charts that patient's own portal login can act on through family portal access, each linked for staff to open.
- The background job that moves notes to the new note format no longer notifies plugins about the notes it moves, and it leaves a note in its original format instead of dropping a command it cannot convert.
