Today's release includes the following updates:

<span class="tag-portal">portal</span>

- **Family portal access is here!** Staff can give a patient's family member or caregiver, such as a parent, guardian, or adult child, their own login to the patient portal for that patient's chart. See [Family Portal Access](https://help.canvasmedical.com/articles/1380397114-family-portal-access).
  - **Access per area:** from the patient's **Profile → Portal**, set appointments, messaging, clinical, and billing to None, View, or Full.
  - **One login, several charts:** a parent who is also a patient switches between their own record and each child's. The parent's own Portal section lists the charts their login can open.
  - **Notifications:** family members hear about new messages, lab results, statements, and appointment reminders in the areas they can see.
  - **Clear attribution:** messages, bookings, and cancellations a family member makes read as them acting for the patient, such as "Jordan Rivera, for Maya".
  - **Staff follow-up:** when a family member says they don't know the patient, the care team lead gets a task to review the access.

<span class="tag-config">config</span>

- New Constance config `FAMILY_PORTAL_ACCESS` that lets staff give family members and other representatives portal access to a patient's chart.
  - Reach out to Canvas support to enable it for your instance.

<span class="tag-sdk">sdk</span>

- Plugins can now serve family members in the patient portal. See [Family Portal Access](/sdk/patient-portal/#family-portal-access).
  - **Data:** read the [portal access a family member has](/sdk/data-portal-access-grant/) on a chart, area by area.
  - **SimpleAPI and WebSocket:** see [who is acting on the chart](/sdk/handlers-simple-api-http/#family-portal-access) and declare the access each route needs.
  - **Applications:** tell the patient from a family member in a [portal menu application](/sdk/handlers-applications/#who-sees-an-application).

<span class="tag-api">api</span>

- The FHIR API names a family member acting through family portal access as their **RelatedPerson**.
  - [Communication](/api/communication/): a message's `sender` can be a RelatedPerson, for read, search, and create.
  - [Provenance](/api/provenance/): `agent.who` can be a RelatedPerson.
