Today's release includes the following updates:

<span class="tag-bugfix">bugfix</span>

- Fixed an issue where patients who opened a portal invite link could not create their account. Selecting Create Account grayed out the button and the account was never created. Patients can now create their account from the invite link.
  - A patient who got stuck can open the same invite link again to finish.
  - If signing out of the portal fails, the portal now says so instead of reporting success.
- Fixed an issue where team chat intermittently timed out while loading contacts and unread counts. Contacts and unread counts now load reliably.
- Fixed an issue where opening a command permalink could scroll the linked command out of view while the rest of the note loaded. The linked command now stays on screen while the remaining note content loads.

<span class="tag-ui">ui</span>

- Clinicians can now connect their own Google account from a new **Google Calendar** item in the provider menu.
  - Canvas copies their appointments to a "Canvas (Busy)" calendar in their Google account. Each event shows the visit type only, with no patient details.
  - Busy time on the Google calendars they choose blocks their availability in Canvas and shows as a busy block on the schedule.
  - Changes sync about every 15 minutes.
  - Disconnecting removes the "Canvas (Busy)" calendar from their Google account and releases the busy time in Canvas.

<span class="tag-config">config</span>

- Two new instance-wide Constance config settings control Google Calendar sync. Both are off by default; reach out to Canvas support to enable them for your instance.
  - `GOOGLE_CALENDAR_ENABLED` turns on the Google Calendar page and sync described above.
  - `GOOGLE_CALENDAR_PUSH_ENABLED` brings busy time from Google into Canvas within moments of a change, instead of on the 15-minute sync. Appointments copied to Google stay on the 15-minute sync.

<span class="tag-sdk">sdk</span>

- Plugins can now show [notification badges](/sdk/effect-application-notification-badge/) on `portal_menu_item` applications, next to their label in the patient portal menu.
  - The count is set when the portal loads, from the application's `compute_notification_badge()`, which receives the signed-in patient.
  - A badge update sent with `patient_ids` and no `staff_ids` reaches each listed patient in the portal right away.
