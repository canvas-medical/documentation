Today's release includes the following updates:

<span class="tag-ui">ui</span>

- Claims can now be assigned to a staff member or a team.
  - Pick an owner in the new **Assignee** field in the claim header.
  - See who owns each claim in a revenue queue, and filter or sort by owner, including **Unassigned**.
    - To show the column, go to **Settings > Quality & Revenue > Queues**, open the queue, add **Assignee** to its **Visible Columns**, and save.
  - Every owner change, whether made in Canvas or by a plugin, is captured in the claim's activity log.
  - Pair it with a plugin to route claims for you: a plugin can [assign each new claim](/sdk/effect-claims/#assign) to your billing team or to the note's provider, and [react when an assignment changes](/sdk/events/#claims). Plugin assignments show in the same **Assignee** field and queue column, and plugins can read [who a claim is assigned to](/sdk/data-claim/#claim).

<span class="tag-portal">portal</span>

- Fixed an issue where a telehealth visit in the patient portal lost its **Join** button as soon as its start time passed. A visit now stays under Appointments, with **Join**, until its scheduled end.

<span class="tag-bugfix">bugfix</span>

- Fixed an issue where a command added from the chart to a note with no line selected landed at the top of the note. It now goes below the note's last command.
- Fixed an issue where pasting into a note line right after typing in it could create a Clipboard command that didn't appear in the note. The pasted text now shows as a Clipboard command on that line.
