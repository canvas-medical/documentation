Today's release includes the following updates:

<span class="tag-bugfix">bugfix</span>

- Fixed an issue with Google Calendar Sync where recurring event end dates were not being properly saved.
- Fixed an error that could occur when changing the state of newer "v2" notes (for example, signing or locking) through the notes API.

<span class="tag-sdk">sdk</span>

- The Add New Patient (Quick Add) form now includes an optional Email field with a toggle to send the patient an email verification link. Entered emails are format-validated and saved to the patient's contact points at creation.
