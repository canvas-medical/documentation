Today's release includes the following updates:

<span class="tag-bugfix">bugfix</span>

- Fixed an issue where appointments a plugin created or rescheduled showed the appointment's provider, rather than the user who made the change, in the scheduling history. Plugin-created appointments also showed a duplicate scheduling entry. The history now names the user whose action triggered the plugin, and each step appears once.

<span class="tag-sdk">sdk</span>

- Two new [embedded applications](/sdk/handlers-embedded-applications/) let plugins control whether a provider menu item or panel bar icon appears for each user, patient, and page, using a [`visible()`](/sdk/handlers-embedded-applications/#visibility) method:
  - [`ProviderMenuApplication`](/sdk/handlers-embedded-applications/#provider-menu-applications) places an entry at the top or bottom of the provider menu.
  - [`PanelApplication`](/sdk/handlers-embedded-applications/#panel-applications) places an icon in the panel bar or the app drawer, inside or outside a patient chart.
