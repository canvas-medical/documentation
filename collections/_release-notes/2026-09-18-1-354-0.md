Today's release includes the following updates:

<span class="tag-bugfix">bugfix</span>

- Fixed an issue where a lab report from Health Gorilla that could not be matched to a staff member by NPI went to the patient's care team lead for review rather than to the provider who ordered it. These reports now go to the ordering provider on the lab order, and fall back to the care team lead only when the report has no lab order.
- Fixed an issue where a message note created outside of charting, such as through the FHIR [Communication](/api/communication/) endpoint, showed an empty card stuck on a loading placeholder when opened on the patient chart timeline. These notes now open on the timeline. The message itself was always readable in the messages panel.
- Fixed an issue where upgrading a plugin kept [applications](/sdk/handlers-applications/) it no longer declares, leaving orphaned or duplicate entries in the app drawer. Upgrading a plugin now removes any application missing from its manifest, along with that application's icon.
