Today's release includes the following updates:
<span class="tag-bugfix">bugfix</span>

- Fixed an issue where coverages added or updated showed their payer's plan type as Commercial regardless of the actual type. Coverages added or updated after this release now show the correct plan type on claims, and plan types display with their full names.
- Fixed an issue where restoring a cancelled appointment from the patient chart cleared the note's existing content. The restored note now opens with its full charting area.

<span class="tag-sdk">sdk</span>

- The Django admin no longer displays the secret key or QR code of a user's authenticator app. Users set up and use authenticator apps exactly as before.
- Staff limited to certain patient groups can no longer add, change, or remove the known location of a patient outside those groups. Staff who can open a patient's profile edit known locations as before.
- Approving an OAuth app connection now requires multi-factor authentication to be completed first. Users who haven't verified yet in their current session are asked to verify, then returned to the approval screen.
- Patient portal password sign-in now answers repeated failed attempts the same way whether or not the username, email, or phone number belongs to a patient, so failed attempts cannot be used to discover which patients have portal accounts.
- Patient portal password logins now count every wrong password toward the account's failed-attempt limit, even when the request carries another portal user's sign-in token.
- After signing in or completing MFA, Canvas only sends users to pages on their own Canvas site, even when the sign-in link was crafted to point elsewhere.
- Staff can only change a patient's portal sign-in details or send their portal invite when they can see every chart that portal login opens, including charts it reaches through family portal access.
- Patient portal users who can access more than one chart can now switch between them from the left rail. A banner shows whose record they are viewing and that their actions are recorded on that person's behalf. Patients can also see who has access to their own record on their Home page.
- Canvas support staff whose Okta accounts predate email verification can sign in to instances as root again. Root sessions and their audit events name the support person by their Okta login.
