Today's release includes the following updates:

<span class="tag-bugfix">bugfix</span>

- The claim scrubber no longer flags a missing rendering provider Tax ID, which is not required under group/organization billing.
- Fixed an issue where refill and action-button commands could be inserted into another provider's collapsed note. Commands now target the note you currently have open, or start a fresh note when everything is collapsed.
- Changing a claim's Rendering Provider from the dropdown now saves correctly when the selected provider has no Tax ID, instead of failing with an error.

<span class="tag-sdk">sdk</span>

- Added a new support-toggled setting, USE_NOTE_LOCATION_FOR_PRESCRIBER_ADDRESS (default off). When enabled, outgoing NewRx prescriptions use the note's practice location for the prescriber's address, business name, phone, and fax — useful for providers who work at multiple offices and need white-bagged medications shipped to the location where the patient is being seen.
- Plugins can now choose the practice location that supplies the prescriber ("from") address on an outbound Surescripts prescription, on a per-prescription basis, so a provider working across multiple offices can route a specific medication to a specific site. When set, the selected location's business name, phone, fax, and street address replace the prescriber's default on the electronic prescription.
